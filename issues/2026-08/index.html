Insights on the A-word

Benchmarks belong before the data, not after

By Rob Smith

A benchmark set before data collection is what separates a real target from a number chosen to match the result. It is the difference between measuring and rationalizing.

As departments align their courses to the new general education outcomes, this is the habit worth building first: name the threshold you expect, in writing, before the first piece of student work is scored.

OutcomeMeasureBenchmark
Written communicationCapstone rubric80% proficient
Quantitative reasoningEmbedded itemsMean 3.0 of 4

The office will circulate a short benchmark guide, with worked examples, to every department this month.
